# Congress Trade Check

Stock trades disclosed by members of Congress, the President, Cabinet officials and corporate insiders, benchmarked against the S&P 500.

A static site: `index.html` plus its data files (`data.js`, `series.js`, `prices.js`, `photos.js`, `photo_credits.js`, `estimate_test.js`) and `portraits/`. Open `index.html` or serve the folder with any static host.

Sources: U.S. House Clerk periodic transaction reports, Office of Government Ethics Form 278-T and 278e, Senate reports, SEC Form 4. Amounts are estimates within the reported ranges. Portraits are official public-domain photos or free-licence images from Wikipedia (credited on the page).

This is a research tool, not financial advice. A committee match is not evidence of wrongdoing.
